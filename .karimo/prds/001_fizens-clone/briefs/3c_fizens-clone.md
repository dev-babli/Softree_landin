## Research Context

### Patterns to Follow

- **Accordion reuse:** `src/components/ui/accordion.tsx` uses Radix UI primitives (`AccordionRoot`, `AccordionItem`, `AccordionTrigger`, `AccordionContent`). Import and style it with fizens tokens.
- **Blog card pattern:** Category pill + title + date + excerpt + "Read More →" link. Cards in CSS grid.

### Recommended Approach

- Style the Radix accordion with fizens tokens by wrapping it in a `<div className="fizens-faq">` and applying CSS overrides in `fizens.css` (or inline styles on the wrapper).
- Blog cards: `border: 1px solid var(--fizens-blue-light-border); border-radius: 16px; padding: 24px; background: white`.

## Requirements

### Blog section
1. `id="blog"`.
2. Pill badge: "Blog".
3. H2: "Read the Articles".
4. 3 article cards in a 3-column grid:
   - Card 1: "Finance" category · "How to Build a 6-Month Emergency Fund" · May 2026 · 50-word excerpt
   - Card 2: "Investing" category · "5 Passive Income Strategies for 2026" · April 2026 · 50-word excerpt
   - Card 3: "Tips" category · "Understanding Your Credit Score in 10 Minutes" · March 2026 · 50-word excerpt
5. Each card: category pill (blue), title (Poppins semibold), date (gray), excerpt (Geist, gray), "Read More →" link.
6. Mobile: 1-col stack.

### FAQ section
1. `id="faq"`.
2. Pill badge: "FAQ".
3. H2: "Frequently Asked Questions".
4. 6 Q&A pairs using the existing `accordion.tsx`:
   - Q: "Is my financial data secure?" A: We use 256-bit encryption and are SOC2 certified.
   - Q: "Which banks are supported?" A: Over 10,000 banks in 40 countries.
   - Q: "Can I cancel anytime?" A: Yes, cancel with one click from your dashboard.
   - Q: "Is there a free trial?" A: Yes, our Free plan is free forever with core features.
   - Q: "How does AI budgeting work?" A: Our AI analyzes your transactions to suggest optimizations.
   - Q: "Do you support joint accounts?" A: Yes, with our Pro and Enterprise plans.
5. One accordion item open at a time (`type="single" collapsible`).
6. Style accordion trigger to use `--fizens-text-dark` for question text, Poppins semibold.
