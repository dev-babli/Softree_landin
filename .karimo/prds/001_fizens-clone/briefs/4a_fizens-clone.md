## Requirements

### CTA Card
1. Inside a section with light background, render a card with:
   - `background: linear-gradient(135deg, #0040c1 0%, #2970ff 100%)`
   - `border-radius: 24px`
   - `padding: 64px`
2. Headline (white, Poppins bold): "Your First Step To Financial Freedom Begins Here"
3. Subtext (white/light, Geist): "Join 50,000+ users who trust Fizens with their finances."
4. Two buttons side-by-side: "Get Started Free" (white bg, blue text, pill) + "Download App" (transparent, white border, white text, pill).
5. Optional: app store badge placeholders (Apple App Store, Google Play) as styled `<span>` badges.
6. Responsive: text and buttons stack/resize on mobile.

### Footer
1. Dark background: `background: #0a0a0a; color: white`.
2. Top row: logo ("Fizens") left, tagline right.
3. 4 navigation columns:
   - **Product:** Features, Pricing, Blog, Changelog
   - **Company:** About, Careers, Press, Contact
   - **Resources:** Docs, Help Center, Status, Security
   - **Legal:** Privacy Policy, Terms, Cookie Policy, GDPR
4. Social icons row: Twitter/X, LinkedIn, Instagram, GitHub (text or Unicode: 𝕏, in, 📷, ⌨).
5. Copyright line: "© 2026 Fizens. All rights reserved."
6. Mobile: columns stack 2×2, then 1 col.
