2. Pill badge: "Pricing".
3. H2: "Try For Free And Start Controlling Your Finances" — Poppins bold.
4. 3 pricing cards:

   **Free — $0/mo**
   - Up to 3 bank accounts
   - Basic spending analytics
   - Monthly budget reports
   - Email support
   - CTA: "Get Started Free"

   **Pro — $12/mo** ← highlighted (blue bg, white text)
   - Everything in Free
   - Unlimited bank accounts
   - AI spending insights
   - Real-time alerts

   **Enterprise — Custom**
   - Everything in Pro
   - Team accounts (up to 50)
   - API access
   - Dedicated manager
   - Custom integrations
   - CTA: "Contact Sales"

5. Pro card visually elevated: blue background, white text, optionally a "Most Popular" badge at top.
6. Each card: tier name, price, description, feature list, CTA button.
7. Mobile: cards stack vertically.
